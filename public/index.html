Water Futures: What Should We Prioritise?

By 2060, fresh water is scarce. Every drop is contested between keeping people alive, powering technology, and healing the land. There are no right answers. For each triangle below, place one dot to show how you think society weighs these three priorities.

  • Survival — water kept for human life
  • Innovation — water for AI, data, and growth
  • Nature — water returned to land and ecosystems

How to read the triangle: each corner is one value. The closer your dot sits to a corner, the more weight you give that value. A dot in the exact centre means you weigh all three equally, and the three weights always add up to 100%. So moving toward one value necessarily means giving up some of the other two.

For example: a dot near the Survival corner means you think water should go mostly to keeping people alive, with little left for technology or restoring the land. A dot halfway between Survival and Nature means you split water between human life and healing ecosystems, while giving almost nothing to innovation.

Responses are anonymous and collected for a university module project (MIS20070 Digital Society). No personal information is recorded.
